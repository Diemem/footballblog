### 🥈 2. Pelé – 1958 (Brazil)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #dcfce7, #bbf7d0); padding: 25px; border-radius: 15px; margin: 25px 0; border-left: 5px solid #16a34a;">
  <div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; align-items: center; flex-wrap: wrap;">
    <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1574629810360-7efbbe195018?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=300&q=80" alt="Brazil World Cup celebration" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover; border-radius: 50%; border: 4px solid #16a34a;" />
    <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 300px;">
      <h3 style="color: #15803d; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇧🇷 The 17-Year-Old Phenomenon</h3>
      <p style="color: #166534; margin-bottom: 10px;">At just 17 years old, Pelé lit up the World Cup in Sweden, scoring a hat-trick in the semifinals and two goals in the final as Brazil won its first title.</p>
      <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
        <p style="color: #15803d; margin: 0; font-weight: 600;">⭐ Youngest player to score in a World Cup final</p>

    </div>
  </div>

### 🥉 3. Just Fontaine – 1958 (France)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #dbeafe, #bfdbfe); padding: 25px; border-radius: 15px; margin: 25px 0; border-left: 5px solid #2563eb;">
  <div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; align-items: center; flex-wrap: wrap;">
    <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1508098682722-e99c43a406b2?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&auto=format&fit=crop&w=300&q=80" alt="France national team" style="width: 150px; height: 150px; object-fit: cover; border-radius: 50%; border: 4px solid #2563eb;" />
    <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 300px;">
      <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇫🇷 The Unbreakable Record</h3>
      <p style="color: #1e40af; margin-bottom: 10px;">Fontaine's stunning tally of <strong>13 goals</strong> in a single World Cup remains a tournament record that has never been approached.</p>
      <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
        <p style="color: #1d4ed8; margin: 0; font-weight: 600;">🎯 13 goals in 6 matches - Still the record</p>
      </div>
    </div>

### 4. Ronaldo Nazário – 2002 (Brazil)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #fef7ff, #fae8ff); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; margin: 20px 0; border-left: 4px solid #a855f7;">
  <h4 style="color: #7c2d12; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇧🇷 The Phenomenon's Redemption</h4>
  <p style="color: #86198f; margin-bottom: 10px;">Ronaldo scored <strong>8 goals</strong> in the 2002 World Cup — including two in the final — leading Brazil to its fifth title and earning the Golden Boot.</p>
  <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.5); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
    <p style="color: #7c2d12; margin: 0; font-size: 14px;">His return to top form after serious injury added to the performance's legend.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### 5. Miroslav Klose – 2002–2014 (Germany)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #f3f4f6, #e5e7eb); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; margin: 20px 0; border-left: 4px solid #6b7280;">
  <h4 style="color: #374151; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇩🇪 The Ultimate World Cup Scorer</h4>
  <p style="color: #4b5563; margin-bottom: 10px;">Across four World Cups, Klose became the <strong>all-time top scorer in World Cup history with 16 goals</strong>.</p>
  <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
    <p style="color: #374151; margin: 0; font-size: 14px;">His consistency and poise in the biggest tournament cemented his place among the greats.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### 6. Lionel Messi – 2022 (Argentina)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #e0f2fe, #bae6fd); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; margin: 20px 0; border-left: 4px solid #0284c7;">
  <h4 style="color: #0c4a6e; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇦🇷 The GOAT's Crowning Glory</h4>
  <p style="color: #075985; margin-bottom: 10px;">Messi led Argentina to World Cup glory, scoring key goals and providing leadership throughout the tournament.</p>
  <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
    <p style="color: #0c4a6e; margin: 0; font-size: 14px;">In the final alone, he scored twice and was named the Golden Ball winner as the tournament's best player.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### 7. Gerd Müller – 1970 (West Germany)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #fef2f2, #fecaca); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; margin: 20px 0; border-left: 4px solid #dc2626;">
  <h4 style="color: #991b1b; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇩🇪 Der Bomber's Masterclass</h4>
  <p style="color: #b91c1c; margin-bottom: 10px;">Müller scored <strong>10 goals</strong> in the 1970 tournament, defining his era as one of the most lethal strikers in World Cup history.</p>
  <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
    <p style="color: #991b1b; margin: 0; font-size: 14px;">His efficiency and clinical finishing set the standard for tournament goal-scoring.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### 8. Zinedine Zidane – 1998 & 2006 (France)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #ede9fe, #ddd6fe); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; margin: 20px 0; border-left: 4px solid #7c3aed;">
  <h4 style="color: #5b21b6; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇫🇷 Elegance and Controversy</h4>
  <p style="color: #6d28d9; margin-bottom: 10px;">Zidane was dominant in France's run to the title in 1998 with two goals in the final against Brazil.</p>
  <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
    <p style="color: #5b21b6; margin: 0; font-size: 14px;">He also starred in the 2006 final in his farewell World Cup, showing flair and leadership on the biggest stage.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### 9. Kylian Mbappé – 2022 (France)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #fff7ed, #fed7aa); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; margin: 20px 0; border-left: 4px solid #ea580c;">
  <h4 style="color: #c2410c; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇫🇷 The Speed Demon's Showcase</h4>
  <p style="color: #dc2626; margin-bottom: 10px;">Mbappé's electrifying World Cup in 2022 included <strong>8 goals</strong> and a rare hat-trick in a final.</p>
  <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
    <p style="color: #c2410c; margin: 0; font-size: 14px;">His speed, power, and precision made him one of the tournament's most unforgettable performers.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### 10. Roger Milla – 1990 (Cameroon)

<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #f0fdf4, #dcfce7); padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px; margin: 20px 0; border-left: 4px solid #16a34a;">
  <h4 style="color: #15803d; margin-bottom: 10px;">🇨🇲 The Ageless Wonder</h4>
  <p style="color: #166534; margin-bottom: 10px;">At 38, Roger Milla captured hearts and headlines by leading Cameroon to the quarter-finals in Italy.</p>
  <div style="background: rgba(255,255,255,0.7); padding: 10px; border-radius: 6px;">
    <p style="color: #15803d; margin: 0; font-size: 14px;">Scoring four goals and becoming a global sensation with his iconic goal celebrations.</p>
